Kronex Technologies ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Juice Sort mobile game (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use the App.

The App is designed as a game and does not require you to create an account to play. Depending on how you use the App and the advertising services available in your region, the App and its third-party service providers may process:

  • Device information, such as device type, operating system, language, and app version.

  • Game and app interaction information, such as ad interactions, crash information, and diagnostic information.
  • Local game information, such as level progress, settings, coins, rewards, and preferences stored on your device.

2. Advertising

Juice Sort displays advertisements and may use Google AdMob and Google Mobile Ads mediation. Depending on your mediation configuration and availability, advertising may be served or measured by third-party advertising partners, including Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, AppLovin, and Mintegral.

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies for purposes such as serving and measuring advertisements, limiting ad frequency, preventing fraud, and, where permitted, providing personalized advertising.

Advertising partners and configurations can change over time. We recommend that users review the applicable privacy information provided by the advertising services available in their region.

We may share information with service providers and advertising partners that help operate the App, including advertising, measurement, security, and technical service providers.
