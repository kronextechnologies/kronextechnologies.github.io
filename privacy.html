LockIn Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026

LockIn is developed by Kronex Technologies.

Local productivity information

Plans, notes, focus sessions, routine results, goals, streaks, scores, selected themes, selected fonts, and alarm settings are stored locally on the user's device. This version does not provide a Kronex account system and does not upload those productivity records to a Kronex server.

Advertising

LockIn uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and advertising information to provide, secure, measure, and personalize advertising where permitted.

Consent

Where required, LockIn uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage advertising consent. Eligible users can reopen advertising privacy choices from Settings.

Permissions

LockIn may request notification, exact alarm, vibration, wake lock, foreground media playback, network, and motion-sensor access. These permissions support reminders, alarms, advertisements, and pickup detection.

Children and audience

LockIn is a general productivity tool and does not intentionally request a child's name, email address, precise location, or account information. Ad requests are configured for content suitable for general audiences.

Deletion

Users can remove locally stored LockIn data through Settings → Reset all app data. Uninstalling the application also removes app-local data, subject to Android backup behavior.
