Juice Sort.

A casual liquid-sorting puzzle game built around simple controls, colorful presentation, and progressively challenging gameplay.

Android · Under closed testing
The game

Sort. Solve. Keep going.

Juice Sort is designed as an accessible puzzle experience where players organize colored liquids into bottles. The game combines straightforward interactions with level progression and optional in-game rewards.

Liquid sortingOrganize colored liquids across bottles using simple puzzle interactions.
Level progressionProgress through game levels and keep your locally stored game progress.
Coins & rewardsGameplay can include coins, rewards, hints, and extra bottles as virtual items.
ThemesOptional themes and gameplay preferences can be stored locally on your device.
Rewarded adsOptional game benefits may be offered after successfully completing a rewarded advertisement.
Casual playNo account is required to play the game.
