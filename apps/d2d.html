D2D

A connected commerce concept built to coordinate the journey from product discovery and selling to order progress and delivery.

In development

One ecosystem. Different roles. One connected journey.

D2D is being designed around dedicated experiences for buyers, sellers, and delivery operations while keeping the order journey connected.

Buyer experienceDiscover products, place orders, and follow order progress from purchase to delivery.
Seller toolsManage products, inventory, customer orders, and shipment progress from a dedicated workflow.
Delivery visibilitySupport clear handoffs and shipment status updates as parcels move through the delivery process.
Connected communicationKeep the people involved in an order informed through structured status and messaging flows.
