The product

A practical system for getting the day under control.

LockIn brings planning, focus sessions, reminders, completion tracking, and performance review into one experience.

Daily planningBuild a clear routine and organize what needs to happen throughout the day.
Focus sessionsSet a duration, start the session, and keep attention on the task in front of you.
Smart remindersUse timely reminders and alarms to stay aware of upcoming tasks and start times.
Completion trackingRecord what was completed, skipped, ended early, or left unfinished.
Performance insightsReview recent activity, consistency, timing, streaks, and progress.
PersonalizationShape the experience with themes, fonts, motivation, and useful home-screen widgets.
